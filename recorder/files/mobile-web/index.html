Recordings

BMW’s built-in Drive Recorder on iDrive 7 saves short clips. BimmerVision adds continuous dashcam recording using the cameras already in your BMW.

Record continuously to USB
On compatible iDrive 7 BMWs, the car app records up to four camera views directly to a USB drive. Pressing the horn protects footage from just before and after the honk. BimmerVision records independently of BMW Drive Recorder and has been tested with the factory feature disabled. Recording on a BMW without a Drive Recorder subscription is still unverified.
See what the cameras alone can’t show
BimmerVision saves extra driving data alongside the footage, including turn signals, brake and accelerator activity, steering, and speed when available. After a crash, that data can help show when you signaled or braked and support your account of what happened.
Review drives in your browser
Open Recordings on your phone or computer. Choose your BMW, plug in the USB drive, or open clips you backed up to Google Drive.
